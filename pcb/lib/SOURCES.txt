Project footprints (pcb/lib/greycode.pretty) and where they come from.

MX_Hotswap.kicad_mod: copied from ~/Projects/macropad (drawn there from the Kailh CPG151101S11 dimensions).
SK6812-side-a.kicad_mod: copied from ~/Projects/macropad, which took it from DangerousPrototypes/BusPirate5-hardware,
  MIT License, with the body outline moved from the silkscreen to F.Fab. Pin order MEASURE.
Hole_M2.kicad_mod: copied from ~/Projects/macropad (plain 2.2 mm unplated hole). The board.toml keep-out is a rule
  area that pcb/gen/layout.py adds, not part of the footprint.
SK6812MINI-E_Reverse.kicad_mod: drawn for this project. Pin functions 1 VDD, 2 DOUT, 3 GND, 4 DIN agree with the
  OPSCO and Mutipower pin tables. Pad size, pad positions, the 3.5 x 3.0 mm cut-out and the corner pin 1 lands on
  when the part is mounted upside down are guesses from community footprints: MEASURE against the datasheet.
QFN-56-1EP_7x7mm_P0.4mm_EP3.2x3.2mm_ViaWindows.kicad_mod: generated by pcb/gen/layout.py from KiCad's stock
  Package_DFN_QFN:QFN-56-1EP_7x7mm_P0.4mm_EP3.2x3.2mm (KiCad 9.0.9 library, CC-BY-SA 4.0 with exception).
  Changed: the four exposed-pad paste squares become the five windows in layout.EP_PASTE_WINDOWS, with the four
  ground vias between them. The copper pads are checked equal to the stock footprint's on every build.
  Do not edit by hand.
